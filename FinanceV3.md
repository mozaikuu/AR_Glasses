- Change H, S, C, Sp, D first.
- Recompute COS using: 3,000 x H.
- Keep all template lines, but update any section subtotal and then totals.
- If grant/investment arrives, enter it in CASH IN lines and re-run totals.
- If you switch to all-10 full-time salaries, replace staff lines and recalculate fixed OPEX.
